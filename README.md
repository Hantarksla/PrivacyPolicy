# CopyCam 隱私權政策

CopyCam 是一款在手機上疊加參考圖來拍照的相機 App。你的照片和參考圖只保存在這台裝置和相簿裡，我們不會上傳照片。

我們使用 Firebase Analytics，用來了解 App 的開啟次數、功能使用和付費牆操作，以便改進產品。這項分析包含匿名的安裝識別碼、由網路位址推算的粗略地區，以及產品互動事件。我們不收集姓名、照片或廣告識別碼，也不把這些資料用來跨其他公司的 App 或網站追蹤你。

## Privacy Policy for CopyCam

CopyCam is a camera app that overlays a reference photo on the camera. Photos and reference images stay on your device and in your photo library. We do not upload them.

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how often the app is opened, which features are used, and how the paywall is used, so we can improve the product. This includes an anonymous installation identifier, an approximate region derived from the network address, and product interaction events. We do not collect your name, photos, or an advertising identifier, and we do not use this data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.
